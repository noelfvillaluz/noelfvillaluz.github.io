Beyond Checklist Compliance: Risk-Based Project Quality Management

From procurement through commissioning and handover · 22 November 2026

About this publication

Inspection and Test Plans should not assign risk levels in isolation. A controlled project risk procedure and activity risk matrix must first assess activities across procurement, fabrication, construction, pre-commissioning, commissioning and handover.

This article explains the sequence from approved scoring rules to a central risk register, and then to the PQP, work procedures, ITPs and ITRs. It includes a worked 13.8 kV switchgear example and an illustrative activity risk table.
