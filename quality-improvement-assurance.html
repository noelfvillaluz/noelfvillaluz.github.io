PQN Effectiveness Measurement: Link the NCR to the applicable proactive notification and compare occurrences before and after issuance. A recurrence after PQN is retained as evidence for effectiveness review and possible escalation.

NCR Management · Controlled Approval Workflow

NCR Email Routing, Review & Approval

The NCR is routed sequentially. It starts with the QC Inspector, proceeds through the configured QC approval workflow, and is released to the corrective-action department only after the final required QC approval.

StageFromToPurposeStatusAction
1QC InspectorQC SupervisorSubmit NCR for review and approvalDraft / Pending Submission
2QC SupervisorQC ManagerForward approved NCR for management approvalLocked — Awaiting Stage 1 Approval
3QC ManagerCorrective Action DepartmentIssue approved NCR for response and corrective actionLocked — Awaiting Final QC Approval
Release control: The corrective-action department cannot receive the NCR until all configured QC approval stages are approved. QC Supervisor and QC Manager stages are configurable to suit the company approval workflow.
NCR Management · Recipient Response

Recipient Corrective Action & Initial Root Cause Response

The designated NCR recipient / responsible department may provide the initial root cause, correction and proposed corrective action. The final root cause is subject to QC review, verification and acceptance.

NCR Management · QC Control

QC Review & Final Root Cause

QC reviews the recipient's initial root cause and proposed corrective action. The final root cause is completed and controlled by QC after review of the response and objective evidence.

Quality Improvement & Assurance

Proactive Quality Notification (PQN) Management

Manage preventive quality notifications for potential violations or quality concerns requiring action before they become actual non-conformances / violations.

PQN Management FunctionPurpose
PQN GeneratorCreate, save, edit, view and print formal Proactive Quality Notifications.
PQN RegisterMaintain issued, open, under-monitoring, effective and escalated PQNs.
Preventive Action TrackingTrack action required to prevent the potential concern from becoming an actual violation.
Monitoring & Follow-UpMonitor recurrence or development of the potential issue during the defined review period.
PQN Effectiveness VerificationVerify whether preventive action successfully avoided an actual non-conformance / violation.
PQN → NCR EscalationLink to NCR Management only when an actual non-conformance / violation subsequently occurs.
PQN Trend AnalysisAnalyze preventive notifications by discipline, area, activity, category, risk and responsible team.
Proactive Quality

Proactive Quality Notification (PQN) Generator

Generate a formal proactive notification for emerging, repeated or high-risk quality concerns before escalation to a formal NCR. Each PQN remains traceable to subsequent observations and NCRs for effectiveness measurement.

Effectiveness: Not yet evaluated. Monitor recurrence after issuance and compare with the pre-PQN baseline.
